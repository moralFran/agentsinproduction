# Pricing update (effective January 1, 2027)
This page supersedes the pricing page for all new and existing customers.
- **Free**: unchanged.
- **Starter**: $39 per month, 250,000 events per month, 25 endpoints, 30-day retention.
- **Growth**: $179 per month, 2,000,000 events per month, unlimited endpoints, 30-day retention.
Overage on paid plans is billed at $0.60 per 1,000 events. Annual billing gives a 10% discount.
Customers on the old prices are migrated automatically on their next renewal.
