# Pricing
All prices are in USD.
- **Free**: 10,000 events per month, 2 endpoints, 3-day event retention.
- **Starter**: $29 per month, 250,000 events per month, 25 endpoints, 30-day retention.
- **Growth**: $149 per month, 2,000,000 events per month, unlimited endpoints, 30-day retention.
Overage on paid plans is billed at $0.40 per 1,000 events. Annual billing gives a 17% discount.
