Required checks only (validator, E10 + E18). The blind judging (E21) has not been done yet: "passed" here is not
"fix".

**Revalidation after the collection (16/11, `criteria.md` E22 and E23).** The 32 attempts and the 16 controls were
validated again with a stricter rule (E22: a required case in a file that did not finish clean does not count as
passed). The 16 controls came out as expected and 31 of the 32 attempts kept their verdict. T1-A of repetition 2
dropped from passed to failed: in one of the 3 samples the test file was ended by the 300 s per-file limit before
reporting any case (in the other two it passed in full). **After seeing that**, E23 was created (approved by
Francisco): a termination by the limit before any case is not a verdict and the validation is redone once. Redone,
T1-A came out passed; the other validation with a termination (T8-A of repetition 1) stayed failed. **The repetitions
of E23 ran with less concurrency** (one at a time, 8 vCPUs) than the revalidation and the collection (4 at the same
time, 32 vCPUs). The numbers below are the final ones and match those of the collection, but one of them (T1-A,
repetition 2) depends on that later rule; without it, the single agent would have 12 of 16. Source:
`results/attempts-c1.jsonl` and the gateway log, on the VM; per attempt in `c1-attempts.csv`.
